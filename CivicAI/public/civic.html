How CivicAI Works

1. Citizens submit public complaints.


2. The system identifies the relevant department.


3. A priority score is calculated.


4. Similar reports are identified.


5. Complaint hotspots are displayed in charts.


6. Administrators update action and resolution status.


7. Citizens track their complaint progress.
